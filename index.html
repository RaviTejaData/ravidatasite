About

I work at the point where messy raw data becomes something a business can actually rely on — writing the ETL, modelling the warehouse, and choosing infrastructure that won't fall over at 2am. Most of my day lives across Python, SQL and one or two cloud consoles, and I like projects where the constraint is real: a slow query, a flaky source, a pipeline that has to run unattended.
